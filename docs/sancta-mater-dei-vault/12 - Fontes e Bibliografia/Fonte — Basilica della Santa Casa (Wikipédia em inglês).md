## Afirmações que a fonte sustenta no acervo — wp-sh-loreto

- The Basilica della Santa Casa is a Marian shrine in Loreto, in the Marches, Italy, widely known for preserving the house of the Holy Family.

- Pious legends claim the house was supernaturally flown over by angelic beings from Nazareth to Tersatto (Trsat in Croatia), then to Recanati, before arriving at the current site.

- The shrine is designated a basilica by the privilege of immemorial status and enshrines the Madonna and Child image of Our Lady of Loreto.
