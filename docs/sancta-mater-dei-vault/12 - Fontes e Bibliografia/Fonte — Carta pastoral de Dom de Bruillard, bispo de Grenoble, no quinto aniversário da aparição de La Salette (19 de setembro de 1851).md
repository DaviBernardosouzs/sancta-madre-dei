## Afirmações que a fonte sustenta no acervo — lasalette-bruillard-1851

- Artigo 1: «We judge that the Apparition of the Blessed Virgin to the two cowherds on the 19th of September, 1846, on a mountain of the chain of Alps, situated in the parish of LaSalette, in the archpresbytery of Corps, bears within Itself all the characteristics of truth, and that the faithful have grounds for believing it Indubitable and certain.»

- Artigo 3: o bispo autoriza a devoção a Nossa Senhora de La Salette.

- Artigo 5: o bispo proíbe os fiéis e o clero da diocese de falar ou escrever contra o acontecimento.
