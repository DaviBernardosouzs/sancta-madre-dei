- The Basilica of Our Lady of the Angels is a Roman Catholic basilica in Cartago, Costa Rica, dedicated to the Virgen de los Pardos, officially known as Virgen de los Ángeles.

- The basilica was built in 1639 and was partially destroyed by an earthquake; Wikidata gives 1924 as the inception of the present building.
