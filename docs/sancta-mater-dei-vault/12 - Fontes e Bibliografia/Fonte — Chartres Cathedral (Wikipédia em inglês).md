## Afirmações que a fonte sustenta no acervo — wp-sh-chartres

- Chartres Cathedral (Cathédrale Notre-Dame de Chartres) is a Catholic cathedral in Chartres, France, about 80 km southwest of Paris, and the seat of the Bishop of Chartres.

- Dedicated in honour of the Virgin Mary, it was mostly constructed between 1194 and 1220.

- It stands on the site of at least five cathedrals that have occupied the site since the Diocese of Chartres was formed as an episcopal see in the 4th century; its north spire is more recent (1507-1513).
