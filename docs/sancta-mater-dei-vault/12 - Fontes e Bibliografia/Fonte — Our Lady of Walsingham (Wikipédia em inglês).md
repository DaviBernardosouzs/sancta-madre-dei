## Afirmações que a fonte sustenta no acervo — wp-sh-walsingham

- Our Lady of Walsingham is a title given to Mary venerated by Catholics, high-church Anglicans and Western Rite Orthodox Christians.

- According to tradition, the title is linked to a Marian vision experienced in 1061 by Lady Richeldis de Faverches, an Anglo-Saxon noblewoman and widow, in the village of Walsingham, Norfolk, England.

- In the vision, the Blessed Virgin Mary showed her the Holy House in Nazareth and commissioned her to construct a counterpart in Walsingham, dedicated to the mystery of the Annunciation.
